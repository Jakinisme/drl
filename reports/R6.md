## 4. Answer: what is the failure correlated with?

**It is an RNG-stream / seeding artefact expressed through wind — not spawn heading, not a
weakened thruster, not the env index by itself.**

Mechanism (code-read + data-read, confirmed):

* `sim/disturbance.py:92-93` seeds `Disturbance._gen` with `cfg["seed_offset"]` (0 in
  `configs/disturbance.yaml`), and nothing passes the run seed into `Disturbance`
  (`envs/asv_env.py:176` constructs it with the config only). The per-env wind/wave draws
  are therefore a fixed function of the env index, replayed identically in every seed.
* env 0 always draws wind **6.73 m/s from 226°** (SW, pushing south/downwind); env 2 always
  draws the batch maximum **7.72 m/s from 242°**. This matches the evidence track ("smooth
  drift ~45 m south away from the goal"): the wind is essentially southerly, so the vessel
  is advected south regardless of its own heading.
* The PPO raw action mean saturates at ±1 from step 0 (env 0: 66% of the first 200 steps
  have |mean| > 0.99; env 2: 53%), but the **applied** action is tiny and constant (surge
  −0.2, sway −0.4, yaw −0.6) because the env's action-rate limiter
  (`rate_limit_per_s 2.0 x policy_dt 0.1 = 0.2 max |Δ| per step`) clamps a saturated
  command to a crawl it cannot build out of from a zero start. The policy asks for full
  authority and gets ~0.2.
* env 2's 7.72 m/s exceeds what that near-zero authority can counter, so it is blown past
  the 80 m geofence every seed. env 0's 6.73 m/s is enough to stop it reaching waypoint 1
  but not enough to exit the fence.
* PID does not show the env-index *shape*: the wind is shared (PID env 0/2 draw the same
  6.73/7.72 m/s), and PID also scores 0 on its own env 0, but it never geofence-exits like
  PPO env 2. PID commands directly instead of through a saturated, rate-limited policy
  head, so its failures are diffuse (~0.69 waypoints over all envs) rather than index-bound.

No spawn-heading or weakened-thruster signal: those columns correlate at |corr| < 0.3 and
vary normally across seeds, so they cannot explain a per-env, seed-invariant failure.

## 5. Candidate fixes (NOT applied here — envs/sim are out of scope)

1. **Seed the disturbance with the run seed.** Pass the eval/run seed into `Disturbance`
   (add a `seed=` kwarg and seed `_gen` with it, instead of the constant `seed_offset`), so
   wind/waves vary across seeds like spawn and physics do. This is the root fix: the
   "index-bound" failure is an artefact of `seed_offset=0`, not a property of env 0/2.
2. **Reconcile the action-rate limiter with a saturating policy.** The policy sits at ±1
   while the limiter holds the applied command at ~0.2; the two fight. Options: raise
   `rate_limit_per_s`; seed `prev_action` at reset so the first step isn't clamped to ±0.2;
   or ramp the policy mean rather than the applied delta. Env-side (`envs/asv_env.py`),
   out of this task's ownership.
3. Fix (1) makes env 0/2 unremarkable; fix (2) is what actually raises lap completion,
   because a wind-sensitive policy with 0.2/step authority will still lose waypoints.
