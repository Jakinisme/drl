## 3. PID baseline inside the env (measurement, not a test)

`python tests/test_integration.py` — 16 envs, 1200 policy steps = one 120 s episode,
seed 0, physics randomisation **off**, disturbance level 0, `PIDBaseline` at its faithful
defaults (0.05 output deadband, gains copied from `pi-control/src/config.py`). Metrics are
computed per env with `evalkit/metrics.py` and averaged over the 16 envs.

| metric | station keeping | waypoint |
|---|---|---|
| radial error mean | **45.54 m** | **39.24 m** |
| radial error p95 | **93.96 m** | **84.36 m** |
| cross-track RMSE | 47.37 m (RMS distance to the hold point — no leg exists in SK) | 25.12 m |
| settled (tol 2.0 m, hold 5 s) | **0 %** of envs | **0 %** of envs |
| energy (normalized command²·s) | 272.4 | 223.4 |
| action rate | 1.416 1/s | 1.436 1/s |
| batch-mean radial error, first 1 s → last 6 s | 40.03 → **48.39 m** | 38.79 → **40.90 m** |

**The PID does not converge in this simulator; the error grows.** Cause, measured:

- The actuator map is not axis-decoupled and, worse, **every** body command produces forward
  thrust. Computed from `configs/vehicle.yaml` through the §4.2 chain (nominal gains, after
  deadband): `[1,0,0]` → tau `(17.32, -10.39, -1.56)`; `[0,1,0]` → `(17.32, +10.39, -7.80)`;
  `[0,0,1]` → `(17.32, -10.39, +7.80)` N / N / N·m. The surge component is identical for all
  three inputs, so any sway/yaw correction costs ~17 N of unwanted forward thrust.
- Consequence for the PID: the heading loop (gains in degrees, `kp = 1.0`, output limited to
  ±1) saturates, yaw flips sign every policy step, and `u` reverses with it — a limit cycle.
  Straight-line check, hold point 10 m away on the +x axis, hull at rest: distance 10.0 →
  5.88 m in 16 s and then back to 6.01 m with `yaw = -1.0` constantly.
- This is **not** a code bug: `tests/test_thrusters.py::test_body_axes_are_independent`
  already asserts the map is rank 3 and only that yaw is dominant — A1 documented the
  coupling (reports/A1.md open issues 1-2, INSPECTION H6). The magnitudes are placeholder
  `thrust_dir` / `positions_m` (TODO SYSID). **Open decision, not changed by me:** the sim
  needs a bench-verified per-motor `thrust_dir` / `signs.motor_sign` (or an explicitly
  documented "the firmware matrix as written produces this" statement) before PID-vs-PPO
  numbers in Wave 4 mean anything.

Caveat on the numbers: `AsvEnv.step` auto-resets on `done` (CONTRACT §4.5), and p95 is near
the 80 m geofence, so part of each trace is a new episode after a geofence hit. The PID
trajectory is therefore not one clean episode.

20 M-step curriculum. The policy is not being measured for quality — average reward is
dominated by random spawn distance, and no evaluation against the PID has been run (Wave 4).

## 5. The two regression pins asked for

**(a) estimator error on the first step after reset** — `test_estimator_error_small_at_first_step`:
256 envs, randomisation on (the R3 path). After `reset()`: est-vs-truth max **1.95 m**, mean
< 1.5 m, spawn distance mean 23.0 m — i.e. the GPS noise floor, not the spawn offset. After
one more `step()`: max still **1.95 m**. Before R3 this was 38.1 m. The test asserts < 5 m and
< 25 % of the mean spawn distance, on both the reset and the first-step sample.

**(b) effective drag factor over 1000 sampled episodes** —
`test_drag_factor_of_1000_sampled_episodes_inside_config_range`: 2 × 1000-env resets
(2000 draws), `D_lin_env/D_lin` and `D_quad_env/D_quad`, all three axes:
min **0.7010**, max **1.2997**, inside `configs/env.yaml` `randomization.drag` 0.30 →
**[0.70, 1.30]**. The same test asserts mass no longer leaks into drag (R3), so a
re-introduction of the R2 coupling fails it. `tests/test_env.py` now enforces the same bound
on `D_lin` **and** `D_quad`.

## 6. Open decisions (reported, not changed)

1. **Reward weights, geofence 80 m, arrival radius 2.0 m, slowdown 5 m** — untouched, still
   TODO T7/T8 placeholders. With the PID unable to hold station, none of these can be tuned
   from these numbers.
2. **Actuator geometry** (§3): every command adds ~17 N surge. Full training on top of this
   will teach the policy to fight the mixer. Decide the `thrust_dir` / `motor_sign` values
   (bench test) before the 20 M-step run.
3. **Settling + cross-track metrics assume a converged controller.** Both report 0 % /
   inf here — that is the metric working, not a metric bug.
4. **Auto-reset inside `step()` makes a long trajectory a mixture of episodes.** Wave 4's
   `evaluate.py` must consume `info` terminal states (CONTRACT §4.5), not the obs stream.
5. **`obs.scale` ±5 clip during SK from a 40 m start**: `goal_dx` saturates at 5.0 once the
   error exceeds 50 m; the 80 m geofence means the policy cannot see distance beyond 50 m.
6. Sensor model is still ~40 % of the step time (PROGRESS Wave 2 issue 3); 18.8 k env-steps/s
   at 1024 envs means the 20 M-step run is ~18 min — acceptable, but it will not scale up.
